# Purr & Peace

A cozy, browser-based cat-and-mouse game inspired by the classic chase, reimagined as a friendship. Guide the cat with the arrow keys (or touch controls), collect mouse snacks, and grow your tail without bumping into yourself or the walls.

Choose **Easy**, **Medium**, or **Hard** to change the cat's speed, and switch difficulty at any time. Press **Space** to pause and resume. Your best score is saved in your browser.

The game is deployed to [GitHub Pages](https://anupamnohwar.github.io/osencopilotdays/) whenever changes reach `main`. To play locally, open `index.html` in a browser.
